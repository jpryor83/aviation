What I teach

Discovery flights

Your first time at the controls. We fly a short lesson in an airplane you provide, and I walk you through how a flight comes together, from preflight to landing. No experience needed.

Flight reviews

A flight review is required every 24 calendar months to act as pilot in command. It includes at least an hour of ground time and an hour in the air, in your airplane, tailored to the way you actually fly.

Private and commercial training

Training toward your private or commercial pilot certificate in single-engine airplanes, from your first lesson through checkride preparation.

I don't offer instrument instruction. I'm not a CFII, so for instrument training you'll want a different instructor.
